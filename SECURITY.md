# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

All versions are supported at this time.  Once we hit version 2.0, we'll support current version minus 1.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a vulnerability, send an email to me <at> ianwilson <dot> org and give me about 48 hours.

You could also submit a pull request.  I'll send you some DMS swag or give you a month's membership at your local hackerspace :)
