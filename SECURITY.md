# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

Currently, I'm accepting vulnerability reports for any portion of OpenMakerSuite.  If you see something, say something!

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Send an email to me <at> ianwilson <dot> org.  I do have a day job, so, I'll try to get to your report within 48 hours.  

There's no funding for OMS right now, but, I'll make sure that your contributions are acknowledged with every release!
